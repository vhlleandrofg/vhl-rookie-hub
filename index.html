    How to claim

    Doing a task isn't enough: you claim it on the portal, and an updater reviews it.

    1. Post your task on the forumUse the right section, like Media Spots or your team's press conference thread.
    2. Copy the post's linkClick the three dots at the top right of your post, choose Share, and copy the link.
    3. Open Player ManagementOn the portal, open Player Management and choose Claim Capped or Claim Uncapped.
    4. Fill in the claimPick the week, choose the task type, enter the TPE, and paste your link.
    5. SubmitAn updater reviews it. Until then you can edit it under Pending Updates. Once it's approved, apply the TPE to your attributes.

    Button claims

    Practice Facility, Welfare and Training Camp are buttons on Player Management, with no post needed. Trivia has its own claim button once you answer correctly.

    One post, several weeks

    Each link can be claimed once. For a long article worth several weeks, reply to your own post each week (for example, "claim 2 of 3 for week ending 5/18") and claim with that reply's link.

      Build your player

      Where to spend your TPE so your player actually performs.

      Your first TPE

      Start with attributes that feed Defense, the most important rating. A common first move is raising Deking and Defensive Coverage together: between them they also add Puck Handling and Skating. Centers can add Faceoffs. Every level up to 70 costs just 1 TPE, so nothing is wasted early on.

      Earning less than about 10 TPE a week? Aim for a balanced build of around 200 TPE first, then add to it. Earning more? Work toward the 400 TPE starter build below from the start.

      How skater attributes work

      On the portal you upgrade hybrid attributes like Deking or Poke Checking. Each one feeds one or more of the ratings the sim uses, such as Defense or Puck Handling.

      Levels get pricier as they climb, so spreading TPE across the attributes that feed a rating beats maxing one.

      TPE cost per level

      Maxing a single attribute costs 225 TPE in total.

      Which ratings matter

      Ranked by how much they help, with the attributes that build each one.

      Rules of thumb

      1. Build Defense first. It improves positioning, which helps at both ends of the ice.
      2. Raise attributes in rotation. For example, take Poke Checking to 70, then Defensive Coverage to 70, then Deking to 70, before pushing any past 70.
      3. Keep Scoring and Passing apart, or your player hesitates and produces less. Grape found a gap of at least 5 works; older guides suggest about 10.
      4. Forwards and defensemen can use the same build. Defensemen can lean a little more into Defense, forwards into Scoring.
      5. Build the player you'll enjoy. Staying active matters more than a perfect build.

      Grape's 2025 baseline for skaters. It uses 386 of the 400 VHLM TPE and reaches 70 in Puck Handling, Defense, Skating and Scoring, leaving 14 TPE to personalize.

      Your first TPE

      Split your starting TPE evenly across the four primary ratings: Rebound Control, Style Control, Hand Speed and Reaction Time. Then follow the build order below. Every level up to 70 costs just 1 TPE.

      How goalie attributes work

      Goalies are simpler than skaters. There are no hybrid attributes: the TPE you apply goes straight into the ratings the sim uses, like Reaction Time.

      The same rising cost per level applies, so pushing one rating very high gets expensive fast.

      TPE cost per level

      VattghernCZ's advice for the VHLM: don't take any rating past 80 yet, because levels above that cost 5 TPE or more each.

      Which ratings matter

      Every goalie guide agrees on the same priorities.

      A simple build order

      1. Primaries to 70Rebound Control, Style Control, Hand Speed and Reaction Time. They're cheap up to 70.
      2. Secondaries to 70Skating, Size and Agility. Low secondaries hold back what your primaries can do.
      3. Back to the primariesKeep raising the four primaries until you reach the VHLM cap of 400 TPE.

      Goalie tips

      1. The height and weight you pick at creation have no effect in the sim. Only the Size rating does.
      2. Keep secondaries below your primaries. They support the primaries rather than replace them.
      3. Skip Leadership and Penalty Shot in the VHLM. Their effect is tiny.
      4. Look at the builds of successful goalies on the portal. Copying a proven build is a sound approach.

      VattghernCZ's guide includes a full example VHLM goalie build as an image.

      Sources: My Take on a VHLM Build Guide (VattghernCZ, 2025), Player Build Guide (Gustav), Grape's Ultimate Guide

      Made a mistake? You can fix it

      Both options are in the Player Store. Because the first reroll is free, an early mistake is never permanent.

      TPA Recall

      Take back up to 10 TPA from a single attribute that's at 70 or below, and spend it somewhere else. Costs $500,000; 2 per career.

      TPA Reroll

      Reset every attribute and re-spend all your TPA on a new build. Off-season only; 2 per career. The first is free, the second costs $7,000,000.
