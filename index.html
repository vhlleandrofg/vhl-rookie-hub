Start here

The five ideas that make everything else on this page make sense.

It's a sim league

You create a hockey player, and games are simulated by the STHS engine using your player's ratings. You never play the games yourself.

What you control is how your player grows: you earn points by taking part in the community, then spend them on attributes.

TPE and TPA

TPE (Total Points Earned) is everything your player has earned. TPA (Total Points Applied) is what you've actually spent on attributes.

Earned TPE does nothing until you apply it, so update your player regularly on the portal.

Capped vs. uncapped

Capped TPE is limited to 12 a week, and most of your growth comes from it. Taking Welfare lowers that week's limit to 10.

Uncapped TPE has no weekly limit. Practice Facility, career tasks, fantasy games and bonuses are all uncapped.

Forum vs. portal

The forum is where you post things: articles, graphics, podcasts, press conference answers, and the team locker rooms.

The portal is where you manage your player: claiming TPE, clicking Practice Facility and Welfare, answering trivia, and applying TPE to attributes.

Your path

New players start in the VHLM, the minor league, where builds are capped at 400 TPE. From there you're drafted to the VHL.

Along the way you'll pick up one-time bonuses: 10 TPE when you reach 100 TPE as a first-generation player, and another 10 automatically when you graduate to the VHL.

Everything you submit must be your own work. Under the Code of Conduct (section 1.2), you can't rely on AI to write articles, make graphics or record podcasts for TPE. If you used AI at all, say where and how, and show you still put in the required effort. Graphics may use one AI-generated element, such as a background, and no more.

How to claim TPE

Doing a task isn't enough; you have to claim it. It takes a minute once you've done it twice.

  1. Post your task on the forumPut it in the right forum section, like Media Spots or your team's press conference thread.
  2. Copy the post's linkClick the three dots at the top right of your post, choose Share, and copy the link.
  3. Open Player ManagementOn the portal, go to Player Management and choose Claim Capped or Claim Uncapped, depending on the task.
  4. Fill in the claimPick the week you're claiming for, choose the task type, enter the TPE amount, and paste your link.
  5. SubmitYour claim goes for approval. Once it's approved, apply the TPE to your attributes.

Button-click claims

Practice Facility, Welfare and Training Camp don't need a post. They're buttons on your Player Management page. Trivia is claimed with a button on the trivia page after you answer correctly.

Claiming one post over several weeks

Each link can only be claimed once. For a long article worth several weeks, reply to your own post each week (for example, "claim 2 of 3 for week ending 5/18") and use that reply's link.

Build your player

How to spend TPE so your player actually performs.

How attributes work

On the portal you upgrade detailed attributes like Deking or Poke Checking. Each one feeds one or more of the ratings the sim actually uses, such as Defense or Puck Handling.

Levels get more expensive as they climb, so the same TPE buys less the higher an attribute goes. That's why spreading TPE across the attributes that feed a rating beats maxing one.

TPE cost per level

Maxing a single attribute costs 225 TPE in total.

Which ratings matter

Ranked by how much they help, with the attributes that build each one.

Rules of thumb

  1. Build Defense first. It improves positioning, which helps at both ends of the ice, and every strong build invests in it.
  2. Raise attributes in rotation. For example, take Poke Checking to 70, then Defensive Coverage to 70, then Deking to 70, before pushing any past 70.
  3. Keep Scoring and Passing apart. If they're too close, your player hesitates and produces less. Grape found a gap of at least 5 works; older guides suggest about 10.
  4. Forwards and defensemen can use the same build. Defensemen can lean a little more into Defense, forwards into Scoring.
  5. Build the player you'll enjoy. Staying active matters more than a perfect build.

A starter VHLM build

Grape's 2025 baseline for skaters. It uses 386 of the 400 VHLM TPE and reaches 70 in Puck Handling, Defense, Skating and Scoring, leaving 14 TPE to personalize.

Goalies

Goalie builds weren't changed by the hybrid attribute system, so they're simpler. Put TPE into the four primary attributes first: Rebound Control, Style Control, Hand Speed and Reaction Time.

Then add to the secondary ones: Skating, Size and Agility. Leadership can be ignored, and Penalty Shot is low priority. A common approach is to study the builds of goalies ahead of you on the depth chart.

Finding trivia answers

Trivia lives on the portal: two questions a week, 1 TPE each. The trick is knowing where to look.

The most common trap: the portal's records and career stats are unreliable for older seasons, especially before Season 47. If the portal's answer isn't one of the options, the portal is probably wrong, not the question.
